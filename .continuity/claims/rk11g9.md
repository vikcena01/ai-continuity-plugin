Reviewers read src/ (about 1,200 lines, all of it commented) and skip dist/mcp.js. A compromised or careless push therefore ships executable code past every human check, and the Anthropic catalogue pins a SHA and follows new commits automatically, so it propagates without a further review gate.

At a handful of users this is theoretical. At scale a plugin with an MCP server, two hooks and filesystem access is a worthwhile target. Mitigations that do not sacrifice d13: build dist/ in CI from a tagged source commit rather than from a laptop, so the bundle is reproducible and attributable; and state in SECURITY.md how to report an issue and what the build provenance is. Do NOT solve it by dropping the committed bundle — that would reintroduce an install-time build step and defeat d13.
