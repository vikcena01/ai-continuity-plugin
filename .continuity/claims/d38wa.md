The capture template asks for BOTH directions per c15np: what was said, what Continuity actually recorded, and what it should have recorded. It names 'nothing was recorded' as a valid and most-valuable answer, because reviewing stored claims can never find a claim that was never written. The four audit labels are a dropdown, so reports arrive pre-classified.

Do NOT simplify it into a generic bug form. It asks a lot on purpose; the length is the point.

When reports arrive, treat them as Capture Audit samples rather than only as bugs to fix — that is the dataset x10c4 said was needed before a benchmark is worth building.
