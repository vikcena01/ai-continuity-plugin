Discovered 2026-08-26 while closing rk1-rk5: the CLI and MCP surfaces expose freeze and supersede and nothing else that moves status. Marking a fixed risk 'resolved', or an answered question 'resolved', required editing the YAML frontmatter by hand. That is legitimate under c1 (state is hand-editable plain text) and the git diff stays reviewable, but it means open risks and questions accumulate in the resume forever unless a human opens the files. This is the SAME missing verb as q4 (parked needs_review claims have no resolution path) seen from a second angle, so both should be solved by one `resolve` verb that takes a claim, a terminal status, and a reason — rather than two separate flows.
