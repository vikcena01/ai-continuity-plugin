The Stop hook's second loop-guard is a 10s on-disk time throttle (THROTTLE_MS in src/hook-stop-capture.ts). It makes a runaway loop physically impossible, but it also means two turns completed inside the same 10s window produce only ONE capture check. A rapid back-and-forth where a decision is settled in the second turn can therefore still lose it. Open for q1: is per-turn reliability actually needed, or is at-most-once-per-10s good enough in practice? Do NOT remove the throttle to fix this — it is the backstop that makes a blocking Stop hook safe.
