Validated in real use: instruction-only capture (skill + injected MCP instructions) is unreliable — the model skipped it and leaned on built-in memory. Root cause is structural: resume has a precise moment (session start) so the SessionStart hook makes it reliable, but 'a decision was made' has no hook event. Fix: a Stop hook that fires at end of each turn and forces a capture-check (guarded by stop_hook_active to prevent loops). Makes capture automatic at the cost of one extra model step per turn.
