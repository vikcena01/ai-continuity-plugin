Building dist/ and committing does not change what our own session runs. Two commands, then restart: `claude plugin marketplace update continuity-marketplace` and `claude plugin update continuity@continuity-marketplace`. Without this, we ship fixes we are not running, and our own state becomes evidence about an old build (rk).
