The sequence number keeps ids readable and roughly ordered (the original reason for short ids: `freeze d3`, `why c1` must be typeable). The two-char suffix is what makes them safe for concurrent writers: without it two developers each recording the Nth decision both write claims/dN.md, the same path, and git raises an add/add conflict whose natural resolution discards one side's claim. The suffix is ALWAYS letter-first, because a leading digit would be absorbed by the `^prefix(\d+)` sequence parse and d1+'3k' would read back as d13. Do not 'clean up' ids back to bare sequential — that reintroduces rk2.
