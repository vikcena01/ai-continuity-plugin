Verified 2026-08-25 across the whole surface: the reconciler CREATES needs_review claims (src/core/reconcile.ts) and resume RENDERS them (src/core/resume.ts:33), but nothing resolves them. Store exposes only freeze() and supersede() — no transition off needs_review. None of the CLI's 14 commands accept or discard a parked claim, and there is no MCP tool for it. Parked conflicts therefore accumulate forever and the resume header nags indefinitely. The resolve verb needs two outcomes: accept the newcomer (an explicit human act, since it implies unfreezing or superseding a frozen claim — consistent with d4 making freeze the one human gate), or reject it into the x* set with a reason so it is never re-proposed.
